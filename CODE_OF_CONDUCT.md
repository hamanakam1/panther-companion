# Code of Conduct

Panther Companion follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) v2.1.

## Our pledge

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of
age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to
the maintainers at mike@sjultra.com. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated
promptly and fairly.
