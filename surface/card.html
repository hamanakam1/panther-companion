          How Companion works
          1. Set a goal. Describe the outcome you want. Changing it starts a fresh context; earlier evidence stays in exported history.
          2. Add evidence. Paste a note, a terminal result that already happened, or an intent bundle from Intent Capture. No screen recording starts and no commands run here.
          3. Choose your next check. Read the suggestion and inspect its sources. You decide whether to do it. Use Record outcome to save what you found and mark the check Resolved or Still open; Companion does not execute the check or verify your report.

          Explain again uses the current goal's evidence. Prepare a decision summarizes stored questions. Follow reads only explicitly configured sources until its deadline. Pause stops new reads; resume does not restart Follow.

          Sources & local history

            Use End follow or Pause to stop updates. Closing a browser tab leaves the local service running, so Follow continues until its deadline. Quitting the desktop app stops its process. Restarting a host never resumes Follow automatically. History includes previous goals and may contain sensitive text you selected.
